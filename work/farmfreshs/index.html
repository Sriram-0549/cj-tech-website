01 THE PROJECT

A dedicated storefront for
organic & homemade goods.

Farm Freshs is a home-based brand offering natural and homemade food and personal-care products.

The business produces herbal soaps, shampoos, hair oil, millet mixes, health mixes, instant pongal mixes, and podi and millet products. The project contains 26 products organized into five main categories:

02 CLIENT DELIVERY

From brief
to client delivery.

Farm Freshs started as a home-based product business without an existing website.

CJ Tech designed and developed its digital storefront around the client's product catalog, brand direction and ordering workflow. The project was completed and handed over to the client.

01

Digital Brand Direction

Translating traditional homemade quality and authentic ingredients into an inviting, trustworthy digital storefront identity.

02

Catalog Architecture

Organizing 26 products across 5 distinct categories so customers can browse personal care and homemade food items seamlessly.

03

Practical Ordering Workflow

Integrating direct WhatsApp communication alongside standard web cart and checkout to match local customer habits.

04

Production Handover

Tested and verified across mobile viewports, responsive layouts, and delivered directly to the client ready for commercial use.
